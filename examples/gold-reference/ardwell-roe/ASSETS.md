# Ardwell & Roe — asset provenance

**Every image is AI-generated. None is a photograph of a real building.** The studio and its
six projects are fictional, so there is no real building any of these could be a photograph
of — but the fact that they are generated travels with them regardless, because the habit of
recording how an asset was made is the thing being kept, not the disclosure in this one case.

## Why generated, and what was tried first

`config/capability-providers.json` defers generated media until "a real project records missing
publishable imagery as the measured limiting factor on its visual result, **or the governed
input path cannot meet the Ardwell & Roe asset floor**." That second condition was tested
rather than assumed:

- **Wikimedia Commons** — a proper API with per-file licence, author and source URL, and the
  right choice on provenance grounds. It is an archive, not a stock library. Searches for
  contemporary interiors returned Dutch heritage-survey documentation, technical drawings and,
  for "modern house interior", nine scans of a 1921 Sears catalogue. Two contact sheets were
  built and inspected before this was concluded. The tooling is kept at
  `examples/gold-reference/tools/` because the finding is about the corpus, not the query.
- **Unsplash** — the brief's preferred route and almost certainly the right one for a business
  with no fictional buildings to depict. It needs an API key, which was not available.

## The floor

`ardwell-roe-asset-plan.v1.json` sets it: all required assets present, ≥4 projects with a
primary frame, ≥8 project assets, ≥2 portraits, a brand asset, hero-capable. **Met** — 6
projects with primaries, 12 project frames, 2 portraits, 1 hero.

## Editorial decisions

- `quarry-lane-primary` reads as a shed in a muddy field with foreground clutter. It is
  demoted to the secondary frame and `quarry-lane-link` — the opening between the two volumes,
  which is what the scheme is actually about — is promoted to primary. Recorded because the
  swap is a judgement, not a data fix.
- `tidewell-writing` is slightly diagrammatic and is used only as a secondary.

## Delivery

Optimised to AVIF with a JPEG fallback at four widths by role (hero 2400, primary 1800,
secondary 1600, portrait 1000), each with a 24px blurred placeholder inlined as a data URI so
no frame lands on empty space. **1.62 MB of AVIF across all 16 frames.** Source PNGs are
4–7 MB each and are kept out of `public/`.
