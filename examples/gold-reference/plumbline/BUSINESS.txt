Plumbline — a fictional company, invented for this corpus.

WHAT IT SELLS
Database change management for teams that ship schema migrations continuously. Plumbline
sits in CI, reads every migration before it runs, and refuses the ones that would take a
lock long enough to matter, destroy data that nothing has read yet, or break the version of
the application that is still serving traffic during a rolling deploy.

It is not a migration runner. Teams keep whatever tool they already have. Plumbline is the
review step in front of it.

WHO BUYS IT
The staff engineer or platform lead who has already had the outage. Payment is by seat and
by database. The decision is made by an engineer and signed off by a director, so the site
has to survive a technical read and a commercial one on the same visit.

WHAT MAKES THE SALE HARD
Nobody believes a safety claim from a vendor. The category is full of tools that promise to
catch problems and produce noise. The site therefore has to show the actual output — a real
plan, on a real migration, with the reasoning visible — rather than describe it.

THE COMMERCIAL GOAL
Start a free trial against one database, self-serve, no call. The secondary goal is the
enterprise conversation, which is a different page and a different reader.

CONSTRAINTS ON THE PROTOTYPE
The company, its customers, its people and its metrics are invented. The domain is `.invalid`
and can never resolve. No photography is used at all: every image on the site is a diagram
drawn from data that lives in this repository, so nothing is stock, nothing is licensed, and
nothing is claimed to be owned that is not.
