# Gold Standard prototypes — experimental evidence

**Nothing in this directory is produced by App Builder, and nothing in it is production
capability.** These are hand-built websites for invented companies, made to find out what
decisions the factory would have to be able to make, and measured by the same independent
reviewer the factory is measured by.

Merging them into main means the evidence is kept. It does **not** mean:

- the factory produces work of this quality — it does not, and no factory build has been
  scored under the current rubric at all;
- these styles are factory defaults. `docs/STAGE_A_SYNTHESIS.md` §4 lists the recurring author
  signatures — monochrome with one accent, paper grounds, hairline rules, giant editorial
  openings, restraint as the answer — and says plainly that promoting any of them installs
  exactly the house style `ai-slop-resistance` exists to catch. They are outputs, not
  capabilities.

No file here is read by the generator. Nothing in `templates/`, `packages/composition/`,
`config/visual-directions.json` or `config/layout-patterns.json` references this directory.

## Scores

Every score is from an independent different-vendor reviewer against the rubric in
`tooling/lib/visual-rubric.mjs`, with the bar withheld from the reviewer.

**Scores recorded before #257 are OLD SCALE and are not convertible.** They were produced on
nine criteria with no defined meaning for any point on the scale, by a prompt that told the
reviewer the bar was 8.5. Where a before/after is drawn, both sides are re-rendered and
re-scored on the current instrument — see `GENERATION-BASELINE.md`, which is the only
comparison in this directory that has been done that way.

## Imagery

Every photograph is openly licensed, credited in the prototype's own provenance file, and
never presented as owned by the fictional business. Where a prototype's imagery depicts real
buildings by other architects, its pages say so.
