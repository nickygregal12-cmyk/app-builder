NØRREVÆRK — a fictional architecture bureau, invented for this corpus.

WHAT IT DOES
Conversion, extension and repair of existing buildings in Denmark and southern Sweden.
Sixteen people, offices in Copenhagen and Malmö, founded 2011. It has never designed a
building on an empty site and says so as its positioning.

WHO IT IS FOR
Owners of difficult existing buildings — municipalities, institutions and developers holding
a structure that is expensive to keep and politically hard to demolish. The decision to
appoint is made slowly, by a committee, on the evidence of previous work.

THE COMMERCIAL GOAL
Not a form submission. The bureau wants to be sent a building. Its first step is a paid
two-week condition survey written by a partner, and roughly a third of those end with the
bureau advising against the project.

WHAT MAKES THE DESIGN PROBLEM HARD
The projects are wildly unequal — 334 m² to 18,600 m², a 55× range — and a portfolio that
renders them at equal size is a portfolio that has declined to have an opinion. The site has
to say which work matters by how much room it gives it.

A CONSTRAINT ON THE IMAGERY, STATED PLAINLY
The bureau is fictional, so it has no photographs. Every frame is an openly-licensed
photograph (CC BY 2.0) of an existing building designed by another architect, credited on the
page and recorded in ASSETS.md. The buildings are real; the bureau, the projects and the
interventions described are invented. Nothing on the site is claimed as the bureau's own
photography or as its authorship of the original buildings.
