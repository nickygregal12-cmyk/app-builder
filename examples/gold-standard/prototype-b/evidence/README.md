# Evidence

`v6/` is the capture the final verdict was issued on: five routes at wide, desktop, tablet and
phone, tiled rather than taken with `fullPage`, with tall pages cut into legible panels.

`states/` is the interaction evidence — thirteen real states driven through the browser and
named in `index.json`. Generation 1 lost half a point on interaction-craft because a reviewer
was shown static pages and correctly refused to guess at hover, focus and transition behaviour.
This is the answer to that, and it includes the two the first review here said stills could not
show: a frame taken 90 ms into a 220 ms state change, and the same interaction under
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

`verdicts/v1.json` … `v6.json` are every review issued during the build, kept in full. The
captures for v1–v5 are not: they are superseded images of the same five routes, and the record
that matters is what the reviewer said and what changed in response. The iteration table in
`../PROTOTYPE-B-FINDINGS.md` reads across them.
