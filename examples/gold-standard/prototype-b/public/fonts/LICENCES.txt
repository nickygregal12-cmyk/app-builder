  SIL Open Font Licence 1.1

  Chosen rather than defaulted to. Plex was drawn for engineering and enterprise contexts,
  its condensed cut is the right register for drawing labels, and its mono is a working
  typeface rather than a decorative one — all three matter for a product whose subject is
  switchgear designations and safety documents. It is also, deliberately, not Inter: the
  default grotesque of generated SaaS is itself a signal, and a technical product that reaches
  for it has made no typographic decision at all.

  Self-hosted, latin subset, no external request at runtime. Sans is the variable file
  (weight 100–700); Condensed and Mono are static instances at the weights actually used.
