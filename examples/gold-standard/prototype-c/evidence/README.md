# Evidence

`v2/` is the capture the final verdict was issued on: eight routes at wide, desktop, tablet and
phone — including two of the thirty-six colour pages, because a template that renders a
different ground on every route needs more than one of them looked at.

`states/` is the interaction evidence: nineteen real states driven through the browser and named
in `index.json`. It covers the light box under pointer, keyboard, mid-transition and reduced
motion; the catalogue filters on both axes; the sample box adding, filling and refusing; and the
three mobile recompositions that are the point of the responsive work.

`verdicts/v1.json` and `v2.json` are both reviews issued during the build. The v1 captures are
not kept — they are superseded images of the same routes, and the record that matters is what
the reviewer said and what changed in response.

`../PROTOTYPE-C-FINDINGS.md` reads across the two.
