Chosen against the previous two prototypes as much as for this one. Nørreværk set Instrument
Serif over Inter; Interlock set IBM Plex throughout. Both are austere, and a third austere
pairing would have been an author's habit rather than a decision about a paint company.

Archivo is a grotesque with weight and a width axis, closer to signwriting and to the lettering
on a paint tin than to an interface typeface. Source Serif carries optical sizing, which matters
on a site whose body copy runs over painted grounds of very different value. Warmth and
sturdiness rather than restraint.

Self-hosted, latin subset, no external request at runtime.
