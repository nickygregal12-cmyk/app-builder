# Evidence

`v4/` is the capture the final verdict was issued on: seven routes at wide, desktop, tablet and
phone, including two of the twelve catchment pages.

`states/` is the interaction evidence — twelve driven states named in `index.json`. Fewer than
the two prototypes before it, and that is a property of the domain rather than an omission: a
report is read rather than operated, and inventing interactions to raise a count would be the
thing the anti-slop criterion exists to catch. The ones it has are load-bearing — the grid
readout is how 720 cells become legible and the data filter is how 720 rows do — and both are
captured under pointer and keyboard.

`verdicts/v1.json` … `v4.json` are all four reviews. Read them together: v2 and v3 are a
regression I introduced while trying to satisfy v1's wide-screen finding, and v4 is the
controlled revert that recovered it. The captures for v1–v3 are not kept; the verdicts are the
record, and `../PROTOTYPE-D-FINDINGS.md` reads across them.
